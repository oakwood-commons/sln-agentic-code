<!-- Adapted from mattpocock/skills (MIT License): https://github.com/mattpocock/skills -->

Stop. Your last message didn't land. Re-explain it:

- Give a little context first -- what were you responding to, and why.
- Use plain, everyday language. Avoid jargon, acronyms, and compressed
  shorthand unless you define them inline.
- Short, simple sentences over dense ones.
